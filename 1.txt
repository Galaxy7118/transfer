During the time of American imperialism, the United States took over new lands around the world. The country expanded for a few different reasons, like making money, building up the military, and gaining political power. However, I believe the most important factor in American imperialism was ideological. The United States believed that American culture was better than everyone else's. Whether they were trading, fighting, or teaching, this strong belief was the main reason behind how America treated other countries.
First, America's belief that it was the best allowed the country to use other nations just to make money. For example, a 1900 cartoon from Judge magazine shows Uncle Sam exporting goods from America to China and other nations while stepping over the Philippines. This shows that America used the Philippines as a tool to trade with other nations for their own benefit. Treating another country like a stepping stone proves that the United States did not respect the people living there. They thought American wealth was simply more important than the native people.
Next, this sense of American pride was also shown through aggressive military power. A second primary source is an 1899 cartoon from Life magazine. This source shows Uncle Sam standing in a war-like setting in front of a lot of dead or seriously injured Filipinos, and next to him is a cannon. This was trying to show America's power and as a warning to other countries and people to not wrong America or no mercy will be shown. It was meant to be a demonstration of power and pride, and as a message that we will do what is needed, like take control of the Philippines. Using this kind of violence came from the ideological belief that America had the right to rule over others.
Finally, the clearest example of ideological imperialism was how America forced its culture on others. A cartoon from Puck in 1899 shows Uncle Sam trying to spread American culture and ideas to other countries like the Philippines. In the cartoon, it shows him teaching the "non-civilized" countries they need to be like the civilized white nations sitting behind them. This picture perfectly captures the American mindset at the time. The United States thought its way of life was the only good way, and they used this idea as an excuse to change people who already had their own cultures.
In conclusion, this evidence proves my claim that American imperialism's most important factor was ideological. Even though making money and having a strong military were big goals, they were both caused by the belief that Americans were superior. Whether Uncle Sam was stepping over the Philippines for trade, standing next to a cannon after a battle, or acting like a strict teacher, the real reason was always the same. America's strong ideological pride was the main excuse used to take over other lands.
